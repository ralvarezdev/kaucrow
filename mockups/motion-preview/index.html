kaucrow · motion proposals

A · First loadB · Scene switchC · PhotosD · Interface

Preview of every option from the proposal. These are DOM/CSS stand-ins using the real photos, themes and crow sprite — not the actual three.js scenes — so they show timing, easing and feel, not final pixels. Nothing here touches the app. Each demo has Replay; tick “Simulate reduce motion” (top right) to see how everything degrades to plain fades.

A · First loadWhat you see while the page and the first scene arrive. A1 / A2 / A3 are alternatives; the progress line can be added to any.

A1Quiet splash

Night-sky colour from the first paint (no blank flash). The crow blinks over “Hanging the photos…”, then the splash fades and the photos fade in.

A2Stars come on

The sky itself is the loader: stars light up one by one — the same stars the night-sky scene uses — then the photos appear among them. No cut at hand-over.

A3Photos “develop”

A single warm glow breathes while loading. Then each photo goes from blurred, grey and dark to sharp and in colour, staggered, like a print developing.

nowToday (for comparison)

Empty stage with plain text, then the canvas appears at once.

B · Switching scenesOne stage, five scenes. Pick a mode, then use the scene buttons to switch. In the real app the new chunk loads in the background, so the fallback text below only exists in “Today”.

B1 · B2Scene switch

B2 entrances: night sky photos drift in from depth · gallery frames lower and settle with a small sway · table photos are dealt in · growing the crowd grows out from the centre · dotfiles windows pop in like Hyprland. B3 (photos travel between scenes) needs the shared-canvas rewrite and isn’t mocked here.

C · Photos & lightbox

C1 · C2Lightbox open, close, step

Click a photo. C1: backdrop fades, photo scales up softly. C2: arrows (or ←/→) slide the next photo in; a blurred preview shows until the full image arrives (load delay simulated, since files here are local).

C3Hover lift

Hover the frames. Today only the cursor changes.

C4Gentle idle life

Very slow float on the frames and a slower twinkle. Easy to over-do for a memorial — this is deliberately subtle.

C5Camera glide to focus

Click another photo. C5: the camera eases toward it while the dashed constellation line draws from the old focus. Today the focus just swaps.

D · Interface & tone

D2 · D3Header entrance, phrase, crow

Eyebrow, name, years and phrase rise in with a stagger; the hint pill fades out after your first click on the stage; the phrase crossfades with a small rise; the crow hops when the scene changes (D3). Phrases here are placeholders.
